### 🔨 Projects

#### [Bloop](https://bloop.top) — Private Moments & Live Widget Social Network
A private social app where users share live photos, moments, and updates directly to their close friends' home screen and lock screen widgets.
- **Mobile:** Flutter (iOS & Android) with native WidgetKit / AppWidget integration and background sync.
- **Real-Time & Calls:** 1-on-1 and group voice/video calls powered by LiveKit (WebRTC), instant messaging over WebSocket and SSE.
- **Security:** End-to-end encryption (E2EE) with PIN-based key backup.
- **Backend:** Modular services in Go (Gin), PostgreSQL 16, Redis 7, Nginx Gateway, and Cloudflare.

#### MuaSamViet — E-Commerce Platform
A multi-platform shopping solution featuring customer-facing storefronts, mobile clients, and a merchant management dashboard.
- Full shopping experience with product catalogs, carts, checkout, and order lifecycle tracking.
- RESTful backend APIs designed for fast querying and reliable transactions.
- **Stack:** React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS.

#### HaLa — Community & Real-Time Messaging App
A social communication app centered around low-latency chats and real-time interaction.
- Instant messaging, real-time presence, and feed updates across mobile and web.
- **Stack:** Flutter, Node.js, WebSockets, Redis.
